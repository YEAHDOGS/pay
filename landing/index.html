The plan

Four parts. None of them is a bank.

Pay is a client, not a company holding your money. The architecture is written down in the open, before the code.

  1. 01

    Keys live on your device

    The wallet is non-custodial. Keys are generated on your phone and never leave it. If we wanted your money, we could not take it.

  2. 02

    Handles, not account numbers

    A name like you.pay resolves to a public key, no central registry. The send screen shows who resolved it and how much to trust that.

  3. 03

    Messages are encrypted

    Requests and receipts travel as end-to-end encrypted messages. Relays see ciphertext and routing data, nothing more.

  4. 04

    Settlement is pluggable

    The rail underneath can change, Lightning, stablecoin, whatever is fastest and cheapest. The send flow stays the same.

Why

Their ledger, or no ledger at all.

The old way
  • Your money sits in their account.
  • They see every transaction you make.
  • They can freeze you out, any time, no appeal.
The plan
  • Value settles directly between two people.
  • No central transaction database exists.
  • There is no account to freeze, because there is no account.

Handles and rails are the hard parts. We are working on them in the open.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is Pay live?

No. Pay is pre-alpha, currently Phase 0: vision, architecture, and security model documented, with a scaffold to build on. Nothing moves real money yet.

What will the first version do?

v0.1 is scoped to one thing: two people complete a real peer-to-peer payment, sent to a handle, settled on one rail. Wallet, identity, and encrypted transport come first.

Which settlement rail?

Undecided. Lightning and a stablecoin L2 are the candidates; the trade-off gets documented in the open before the adapter ships.

How do handles work without a central registry?

A handle maps to a public key through pluggable resolvers, your own address book first, then decentralized name systems. The client always shows which resolver answered and its trust level, because impersonation is the top risk.

Do you hold my money? Is there KYC?

No custody, ever. There is no Pay balance and no account to verify; compliance for fiat on-ramps sits with optional partners, outside this project.
