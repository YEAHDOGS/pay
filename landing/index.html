What we're building

Instant, person to person

Transfers settle in seconds, phone to phone. The new P2P rails in India move billions a day this way — Pay brings that speed without the central operator.

Decentralized by design

No bank in the middle, no processor taking a cut, no single switch that can go down or say no.

Pay by handle or QR

Simple handles and QR codes instead of account numbers. Point, scan, paid.
