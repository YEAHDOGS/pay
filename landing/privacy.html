Privacy policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026 · A simple baseline policy, to be expanded as Pay grows.

The short version

This landing page collects nothing. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads.

The notify form

The email form on the home page is a front-end placeholder and is not yet connected to any backend. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored.

Fonts and hosting

This site is served by GitHub Pages behind Cloudflare, and loads the Poppins and Lora typefaces from Google Fonts. Like most infrastructure providers, they may log basic technical request data (such as IP addresses) to operate their services — see the GitHub Privacy Statement, the Cloudflare Privacy Policy, and the Google Privacy Policy.

Changes

When Pay launches for real, this policy will be replaced with a complete one before any data is ever collected.
